## You're welcome to

- Use and share the **official, unmodified** app under its own name and icon (for example, installing

- Refer to the project by name, e.g. “a fork of Over&Out”, “works with Over&Out”, in articles,
  reviews, tutorials and links.
- Build it from source for yourself.

## Please don't

- Publish or distribute a **modified** version (a fork) under the name “Over&Out” or with the Over&Out
  icon, or under a name or icon that's easily confused with them. Give your fork its own name and
  its own icon, and change the bundle identifier (`com.gokulmv.overandout`) so it doesn't replace or
  clash with the official app. Saying “based on Over&Out” is fine.
- Suggest that your version, product or service is made or endorsed by the Over&Out project when it
  isn't.

If you'd like to use the name or icon in some other way, open an issue at
<https://github.com/GokulMV/hush/issues> and ask.
